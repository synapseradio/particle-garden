# Glossary

- *coupling* — a force, fluid, or field influence behind a continuous
  strength whose range includes zero.
- *dormant* — a control whose consumer cannot act right now; it dims, names
  the missing precondition, and stays movable.
- *regime* — a named feed/kill coordinate where the field grows a
  characteristic living pattern.
- *settling* — the quiet indicator shown while the world is still answering
  a move.
