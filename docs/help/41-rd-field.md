# Field Appearance

How the field shows itself on screen, apart from its physics.

- `fieldOpacity` — how strongly the field's pattern glows through the
  scene, colored by the Field Colormap buttons above it. Dormant while the
  field is dark, since opacity has nothing to show until something ignites.
  Interacts with: the Field Colormap buttons; Breath In and Breath Out
  (whether anything is lit); Exposure, Saturation, Contrast and Temperature
  (grade it even with Bloom off). The field tints particles and bends
  trails at any opacity.
