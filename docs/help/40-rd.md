# Chemical Field

The Gray-Scott climate and the couplings in and out of it. The named regime
buttons set `rdFeed` and `rdKill` as a pair.

- `rdFeed` — Breath In, the feed rate F.
- `rdKill` — Breath Out, the kill rate k.
- `rdDeposit` — Secretion Rate; zero means nothing deposits into the field.
- `rdFieldForce` — Scent-following; zero leaves particles blind to the field.
- `climateSpeed` — Drift, tours of the named regimes per minute while
  Weather is on.
