# Chemical Field

A two-chemical reaction runs across the whole world: one chemical feeds in
everywhere, the two react, and the product decays. In the right climates
the reaction organizes itself into spots, stripes, worms, and waves — the
patterns the named buttons visit. Particles feed the field by depositing,
and the field can steer them back.

The named buttons set `rdFeed` and `rdKill` as a pair, because a pattern
needs both coordinates; the Weather toggle lets the climate tour the named
patterns on its own.

- `rdFeed` — Breath In, how fast fresh chemical feeds the field. Dormant
  with `rdKill` while nothing has ignited yet: in that climate the field
  stays dark until deposits or a scatter of spores light it, or until you
  move into a self-starting climate.
- `rdKill` — Breath Out, how fast the product drains away. The balance of
  the two picks the pattern.
- `rdDeposit` — how much every particle lays down into the field. Zero
  leaves the pattern undisturbed by the colonies; each species scales its
  own share in Species Chemistry.
- `rdFieldForce` — how hard the field steers particles along its pattern.
  Zero leaves them blind to it.
- `climateSpeed` — how fast the Weather tours the named patterns, while
  the toggle is on.

Field changes take shape over seconds. The high-feed patterns (Worms,
Coral) need a real deposit to ignite, which their buttons apply on your
behalf.
